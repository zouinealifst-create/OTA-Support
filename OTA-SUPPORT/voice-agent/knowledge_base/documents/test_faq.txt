Q: My internet connection keeps dropping.
A: First, restart your router by unplugging it for 30 seconds. If the problem persists, check if the cables are properly connected. Contact support if the issue continues after restarting.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and you will receive a reset link within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Q: My device is overheating.
A: Make sure the device is placed on a flat hard surface with proper ventilation. Clean the air vents with compressed air. Avoid using the device while charging in hot environments.

Q: I was charged twice for my subscription.
A: This can happen during payment processing delays. Please provide your account email and transaction date to our billing team. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.

Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
A: Log into your account dashboard and go to Settings > Subscription. Click "Upgrade Plan" and choose your preferred tier. The change takes effect immediately and you are billed on a pro-rated basis.
